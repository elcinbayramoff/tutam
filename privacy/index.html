Privacy Policy

Tutam — personal finance tracker

Tutam collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. The Android release does not request the INTERNET permission at all, so the app is technically incapable of transmitting your data anywhere.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. We — the developer of Tutam — receive no information about you or your use of the app. We cannot see your transactions, balances, templates, targets, borrows or reminders. No identifier, advertising ID, device fingerprint, location or diagnostic report is gathered, transmitted or stored by us.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is written to a database in Tutam's private storage area on your device, which no other app can read. Specifically:

DataWhere it lives
Transactions, templates, categories, targets, borrows, reminders, calculator historyLocal database in the app's private storage
Settings (currency, language, theme)Same local database
App-lock PINStored only as a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash, in Android's hardware-backed Keystore. The PIN itself is never written anywhere, and never appears in a backup file.
Fingerprint / face unlockHandled entirely by Android. Tutam receives only a yes/no result and never has access to your biometric data.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Android Auto Backup is switched off

By default Android copies an app's data to the user's Google Drive. Tutam disables this deliberately, for both cloud backup and direct device-to-device transfer, so your financial database is never uploaded anywhere without you asking for it.

Exporting your data

Settings → Export data writes a backup file and hands it to Android's share sheet. This is the only path by which your data can leave the device, and it happens only when you choose it, to the destination you pick. You may protect the file with a passphrase, in which case it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived by PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256. The passphrase is never stored — if you lose it, that backup cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Once you have shared an export, whatever you sent it to (a messaging app, a cloud drive, your email) is governed by that service's own privacy policy, not this one.

There is no INTERNET permission and no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permission.

Notification access, and automatic spend capture

Tutam can read notifications so it can record what you spend with Google Wallet without you typing it in. This feature is off by default. It does nothing at all unless you turn it on in Settings and separately grant notification access on Android's own system screen, and you can revoke either at any time.

When it is on:

  • Only Google Wallet's notifications are read. The app checks the originating package before anything is read or written, so notifications from your messages, email, banking apps or anything else are never examined and never stored.
  • Only the amount, currency and merchant name are kept. These are used to build a suggested entry.
  • Nothing is added to your records automatically. A detected spend waits on the Home screen until you confirm it. You can edit it, categorise it, or dismiss it. (If you explicitly ask Tutam to stop asking about a particular shop, it will record that shop's purchases directly — you choose this per shop, and can undo it in Settings.)
  • The notification text is deleted as soon as you confirm or dismiss the entry. It is never included in an exported backup.
  • Nothing is transmitted. As above, Tutam has no INTERNET permission, so notification content cannot leave your device even in principle. It is not sent to us, to any analytics service, or to anyone else — we never see it.

Turning the feature off in Settings deletes the pending entries and stops anything further from being read. To also withdraw the system permission, switch Tutam off under Android's Notification access settings.

Children

Tutam is a general-purpose budgeting tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR grant you rights of access, correction, erasure and portability over personal data held by a company. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to disclose or erase. You exercise these rights directly and completely: your data is on your device, editable and deletable in the app, exportable through Settings → Export data, and destroyed entirely by uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Tutam adds a feature that changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes on Google Play.
